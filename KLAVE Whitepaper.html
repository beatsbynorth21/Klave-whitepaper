KLAVE

The beat marketplace where every beat is made by a human.

Whitepaper, draft v1 · October 2026 · A product of NORTHHHERN LABS

Abstract

KLAVE is an AI-powered marketplace where producers sell beats and artists find them. Producers keep more of what they earn the more they commit to the platform. Artists get beats verified as human-made. NERO, KLAVE's built-in assistant, helps both sides find the right sound and get around the app.

KLAVE is built by a producer, for producers, and it is pre-launch. This paper explains what it does, how producers are paid, and where it goes next.

The problem

  • Producers lose too much. Most beat platforms take a large share of every sale, and the cut rarely drops as a producer grows.
  • Artists can't trust what they buy. AI-generated tracks are flooding marketplaces, and buyers can't tell what a person actually made.
  • Discovery is noisy. Finding the right beat for a mood or a song idea means scrolling through thousands of near-identical listings.
  • Emerging markets are underserved. Many platforms and payment tools don't work well for producers in places like West Africa.

What KLAVE does

100% human-made beats

KLAVE's position is simple: the beats are made by people. Producers go through a stem-upload verification flow, because someone who made a beat can show the parts it was built from. NERO also guides producers through the process in plain language.

Preview, then buy

Artists hear a preview of a beat instead of the full track. This stops anyone from screen-recording a complete beat and using it without paying. Free-tier beats get limited, watermarked previews.

Search that fits the song

Artists can browse by genre, follow producers, and use the Beat Mood Matcher to find beats by feel instead of keywords. Producers get an analytics dashboard and a shareable Producer Card.

Delivery

After payment, the artist receives the beat through an emailed download link.

Fair pay for producers. Real beats for artists. No guessing which is which.

NERO, the assistant

NERO is KLAVE's AI assistant, powered by the Claude API. It helps producers with listings, pricing, and verification, and helps artists find beats. To keep it affordable at launch, NERO will route simple questions to cheaper models and apply message limits per plan, so it stays available without a runaway bill. NERO is currently in preview and opens to users after launch funding.

The Free plan has an upload cap and no exclusive-rights pricing. Minimum prices apply to every plan: $5 for a non-exclusive license and $25 for exclusive rights, so beats don't get undercut into worthlessness. The lifetime plan is a single payment with no renewal.

Ambassador program

Anyone can become a KLAVE ambassador, not only producers or artists. Ambassadors earn a commission on sales from people they refer, and the rate rises with referrals.

How it's built

  • App: a lightweight web app hosted on Vercel, working in the phone browser.
  • Accounts and data: Firebase Authentication with email verification, and Firestore with security rules that enforce roles and price floors.
  • Payments: Paystack for recurring subscriptions and one-time purchases, with server-side payment verification. Flutterwave and Stripe are planned.
  • Media: Cloudinary through a signed-upload proxy.
  • Security: admin actions use server-verified claims, and bank details stay private to the server.
